 -- ARCTICESCAPE --

Your vessel has become shipwrecked somewhere in the
Arctic circle. As competent seamen you must find
your way to a radio and attempt to call for rescue.

ACHTUNG! WARNING! WAARSCHUWING! ATTENZIONE!

A strange research facility happens to exist at the
location you are shipwrecked. For some strange reason
it has many lasers and other unusual killing
mechanisms (including spinners) that you must navigate.
